# WorldsFinestVRP — Research Vault

Hub for the SVRPTW research line. Last updated: 2026-05-14.

## Project headline

A two-stage solver — **`portfolio_pyvrp_warm`** (PyVRP@8s construction + LinUCB-bandit refinement) — beats PyVRP@60s by **+$110/instance mean** on truly held-out OSM-based asymmetric instances at N=100–500, and wins **83% of canonical Homberger 200-customer academic instances** at half the PyVRP budget. Hits the published Solomon C101 optimum (828.94) within 0.01%.
